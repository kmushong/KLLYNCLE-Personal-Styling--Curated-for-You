About Me

Stop paying retail markup for luxury. Modern luxury pricing isn't about true value — it's inflated to be discounted later. But you don't have time to hunt off-price racks or negotiate with boutique SAs. I do. Through years of exclusive relationships with luxury retail insiders, I leverage our collective buying power. You might only want one perfect bag or two perfect pieces, but I pool my clients' selections into 25+ piece bulk orders. That volume is what unlocks real, insider pricing for you. High-fashion access, without the games. And because a curated closet is just as important as what's coming into it — I also offer The Closet Cure. I audit, organize, and refresh your entire space. What stays, what gets resold to earn you money back, what gets donated — I handle it all. Zero decision fatigue, zero effort on your part. You focus on your career. I'll handle the closet and the access.

My Services

Scroll to explore

Style Consultation

We discuss your daily routine, preferred colors, and style inspirations. This ensures your wardrobe aligns with both who you are and where you want to go.

Closet Audit

We go through your current clothes piece by piece to determine what stays, what gets altered, and what goes. You are left with a functional, clutter-free closet made up only of items you love and wear.

Curated Shopping

I create a personalized shopping list and source versatile pieces. My relationships with select brands can also give you access to exclusive discounts, online or in person.

Resale & Donation

High-quality, gently used items removed during the audit are sorted, photographed, and prepared for consignment or resale.
