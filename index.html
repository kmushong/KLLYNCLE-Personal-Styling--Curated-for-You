The Approach

The Philosophy & The Stylist

Tap to see the moment

Demystifying luxury pricing, maximizing your time.

Let's be honest: modern luxury retail pricing is rarely reflective of actual value. Brands constantly mark up prices only to quietly discount them later. But as a busy professional in Washington, D.C., you don't have the time to scour off-price racks, and you certainly aren't going to spend your valuable day bargaining with boutique sales associates.

That is where I come in.......

The Insider's Advantage

Over the years, I have built an exclusive network and deep rapport with key luxury retail insiders. More importantly, I leverage collective buying power.

When you shop with me, you aren't just placing an individual order. I pool my clients' selections into high-volume bulk orders. While you might only be purchasing one or two perfect pieces, our collective order of 25+ items incentivizes luxury boutiques to extend exclusive, insider discounts directly to us. High-fashion access, without the retail markup.

The Closet Cure

Our bodies fluctuate, we grow, we shrink, we evolve. Your closet shouldn't be a source of decision fatigue or a reminder of past versions of yourself. But let's face it: you have a demanding career to run, and digging through hangers to sort, resell, or donate is exhausting.

I have a genuine obsession with clean spaces, luxury organization, and sharp curation. I will audit, organize, and refresh your space, deciding exactly what stays, what gets resold to earn you money back, and what gets donated — all with zero effort on your part.

My Portfolio

How It Works...

Style Consultation

The Goal: Define your personal aesthetic, lifestyle needs, and fashion goals.

The Process: We discuss your daily routine, body shape, preferred colors, and style inspirations. This ensures your wardrobe aligns with both who you are and where you want to go.

Closet Audit

The Goal: Streamline and organize your existing wardrobe.

The Process: We go through your current clothes piece by piece to determine what stays, what gets altered, and what goes. You are left with a functional, clutter-free closet made up only of items you love and wear.

Curated Shopping

The Goal: Fill the gaps in your wardrobe with intentional, high-impact pieces.

The Process: Based on the closet audit, a targeted shopping list is created. I source versatile, mix-and-match pieces that elevate your everyday style, either via pre-selected online links or an in-person shopping trip.

Resale & Donation

The Goal: Give your pre-loved items a second life and recoup some cash.

The Process: High-quality, gently used items removed during the audit are sorted, photographed, and prepared for consignment or resale — turning closet clutter into a return on investment.
