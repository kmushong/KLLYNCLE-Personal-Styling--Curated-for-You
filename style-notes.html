Notes on getting dressed / 06

Small shifts. Better outfits.

Practical thoughts on using what you own, shopping with intention, and keeping a wardrobe working for real life.

01 / EDIT

Start with what already earns its hanger space

Before adding something new, pull out the pieces you reach for most. Notice the fit, colors, and combinations they have in common. That is a useful starting point for a closet edit and a much clearer shopping list.

02 / OUTFITS

Build a week around one anchor piece

Choose a favorite jacket, trouser, or shoe and make three different outfits around it. Change the layers and proportions before deciding you need another version of the same item.

03 / RESET

Give clothes leaving your closet a next step

Separate pieces to resell, donate, repair, or pass along. A simple destination for each item makes a wardrobe refresh easier to finish and keeps useful clothing in circulation.
